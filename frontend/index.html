Elbow Method & Silhouette Scan

Inertia (Within-Cluster Sum of Squares) & Silhouette score plotted across k = 2 to 10
Optimal K = 2

Methodological Insight: While higher K naturally reduces inertia, the rate of variance explained exhibits an inflection point ("elbow") at k = 2. This is independently confirmed by the global peak in Silhouette Score (0.3117), validating 2 natural operating modes for the UR3 manipulator.

Agglomerative Hierarchical Dendrogram (Ward Linkage)

Hierarchical cluster tree based on Euclidean distance in standardized 19-sensor feature space
Cutoff Threshold: d = 3.8

Interpretation: Ward's minimum-variance criterion progressively merges telemetry states. Cutting the tree at distance threshold d = 3.8 yields 2 predominant super-clusters, matching the K-Means partition.

Anonymized credit card applications submitted to major financial institutions

Instances: 690 Applications Features: 15 Categorical & Numerical Target: A16: + (Approved) / − (Rejected) Task: Binary Classification

Preprocessing & Pipeline Integrity:

1. Categorical Encoding

OneHotEncoder fitted on training set with handle_unknown='ignore'.

→
2. Dual Imputation

Median imputer for numericals, most-frequent for categoricals.

→
3. Balanced Scaling

StandardScaler for numeric distributions; zero label leakage.
